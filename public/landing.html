A SIMPLE, SEPARATE FLOW

Two roles. One independent decision.

Each person has a clear responsibility, and the final decision stays with the Buyer.

01
↗

Helper prepares

The Helper enters the payment request and the details the Buyer needs to review. The Helper cannot approve on the Buyer's behalf.

02
◎

Buyer understands

The Buyer checks the recipient, amount, currency and description, with optional speech and explanations grounded in the recorded details.

03
✓

Buyer decides

The Buyer can decline or record consent with their own passkey in the authenticated application. A passkey does not make a payment.

BUILT AROUND CLARITY

Review the details. Understand the choice.

SaySure brings the important information forward and keeps technical complexity out of the Buyer's way.

01 / REVIEW

See exactly what was requested

Amount, currency, recipient, description, expiry and request status appear before the decision controls.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Ask before you decide

Use clear explanations and optional on-demand speech. Help never approves, changes or hides the transaction details.

03 / CONSENT

Keep the decision independent

The Buyer's own passkey is required for approval in the authenticated workflow. Declining does not require a passkey.

Consent is not payment completion.

This staging release does not initiate PayPal checkout, capture funds, issue refunds or claim a completed payment.
